A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of **Wednesday, 25 November 2026**.

Lead story: a cyberattack hits the CIA and the US Director of National Intelligence. Also covered: Ukraine's drone strikes on Russian ships, the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya, a Houthi drone strike on Riyadh's tallest tower, a hostage standoff in Cartagena, NATO's space-defence vow, South Korea's education minister resigning, the WHO evacuating Ebola staff in DR Congo, and England levelling the T20 series in Australia.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `frontpage.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The image is 1024×1024 and under 1 MiB, and both runs write identical bytes.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# -> out/entry.png, byte-identical on every run
```
